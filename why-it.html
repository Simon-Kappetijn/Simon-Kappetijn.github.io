Why did I ever decide to start coding?

Introduction

I never really knew what I wanted to do. I wanted to do something, I wanted to have a purpose, something I would be doing. But for a long time, I had been ‘floating around’ in career choices to so speak, until I discovered the IT field at secondary school. There i got a friend that introduced me to scratch, an extremely simple way of programming, and that really peaked my interest in a way that no other workfield has done.

The story continues..

So, knowing that this IT thing was interesting, I thought to myself that I wanted to orient myself better in this field, so I took Informatica for the last 2 years, and that eventually turned into my graduation project, where i coded a video game in GDscript. I had a lot of fun making this so i thought it was best to continue in the IT field, since it was the only thing that I loved to do that could be turned into a career someday.

What am i going to do??

After going to a bunch of ‘open days’ in a few different schools, it all pointed towards HBO-ICT in Middelburg, where I could expand my knowledge, become more skilled and broaden my horizons on the thing I loved to do before. For now i hope that all these things will become a career and eventually a life that I could live knowing that I’m doing what I love to do, while helping businesses and anyone who asks grow as an added bonus.

Post scriptum

When I wrote this, i realized that the latter was something that I wanted to do since I was very young. Becoming a medical professional to help people was ‘my childish dream’ as a toddler until I was around 12 years old, like how some children wanted to become astronauts or veterinarians.
