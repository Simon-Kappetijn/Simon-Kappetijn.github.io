Why did I ever decide to start coding?

At the time of writing this, I had a few reasons to go study this subject. The main reason was because I like computers, and i didn't really know what else to do. A few years ago however, in my high school, I did a project with a friend of mine, where we made a video game to test psychological effects on how feedback impacts the player. Since then, I have been excited about this field and thought this was worth trying to go for. In the end, I hope this will work out.
