My interpretation of a SWOT-analysis

strengths

I'm really good in putting a lot of time and effort into things that I find fun or interests me in a way. I'd also say I'm very social and that I can strike a good conversation, and that it doesn't really matter about what. I think that these two points describe me best, and that these 2 points can also contribute to the success of my studies. Namely that I can give my all to the studies and that I can work well in groups

i

weaknesses

My biggest weakness is an antipole to one of my strengths. If i’m not interested or if I do not think something is fun, I can’t bring myself to do it, I procrastinate, end up rushing it and eventually that results in not doing it well I also can’t metaphorically see the grey between black and white. I’m a very literal person, so everything has to be very specific and ‘mentioned in the right way’

opportunities

An opportunity to better myself, to learn, to grow and become a contributing person. An opportunity to go do what I’m happy to do, and be able to live a life because of it, for example by making my hobby my job

i

threats

The threat of not liking something keeps lingering in my mind, because there's always a chance of this happening I’m not sure but I think i struggle a bit with impostor syndrome, so that might hinder me in the future Something out of my control. Changing times, the job market, sickness etc.

Goals

Because I find socializing to be very fun, I'm going to turn it into something that benefits the goals of what this study is going to throw at me, namely working effectively together with other people, to share and to learn from each other. I don't want to be a hindrance within any group project, nor do I want to be a nuisance of any kind, so I want to be the best team member I can be, and help myself and others to become the best we can be.

The plan is also to succeed in this entire thing of course. Eliminating the threats is a first priority for that reason. That's why I want to set as concrete goal of not procrastinating, because I think that's my biggest threat. My plan is to accomplish this by setting reminders for myself, and keeping in mind that I want to finish anything at least 1 week before a deadline, so that I have enough time to review and make it spotless (as far as my skills go of course).
