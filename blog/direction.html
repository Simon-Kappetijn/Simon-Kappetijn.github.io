What am I going to do?

What exactly?

I'm not too sure yet where i want to be heading exactly. Personally I'm thinking software development, which has been where everything has been leading to so far, but I'm also not mentally "locking that in" yet. This ICT program is going to be a good opportunity to explore the rest of what IT has to offer, and maybe change my mind on what I'm thinking right now.

Why Software Development/Engineering?

It has been what I've been familiarizing myself with the most in the past, like my About Me and Programming experience mentions. Building apps, writing code and solving bigger problems that way has been very fun in the past, so why not continue it? If the next year doesn't convince me otherwise of course.

Where exactly?

The exact place or project that I want to be working towards is also not clear to me for the same reason. If i can guess, it'd probably be some IT company working on solutions for whatever. What I am sure of though, is that with what this study offers, I want to be able to do passion projects and learn further on my own. Maybe even make my life easier with the tools I'll create.

Why not the other? (Data Science)

From what I've seen, Data Science is more about statistics and mathematics than Software Development, which is the thing I've been struggling the most in my past. I've also had a few run-ins with Data Science in secondary school, mostly in the Informatica class. In that class that I had there, we did a few things catered to machine learning and SQL, which seems to be a big part of Data Science as well. Compared to the Javascript or C-side of the class, I found the Software Development, i.e. what I just mentioned, much more fun to make and do.
