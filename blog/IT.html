My experience at CarCollect

There was an extracurricular with the HZ ICT Student Assocation recently, that I went to to get a bit of a taste of the IT field of work. In this day, we went there by train, since it was in Roosendaal, which went smoothly. When we arrived, we got a tour of the company building, which was fun to see. The thing that I noticed was that the company feel or vibe was very relaxed, they even had free food and stuff like that. After the tour got finished, we went to a room where we got our laptops set up to do some sort of demo. The demo was basically just showing how they build their website, and also how they maintain it. They showed this to us by letting us follow some sort of tutorial to change some stuff on a dummy website. This was probably all fine and dandy, but it was very challenging to me. It felt like you needed prior knowledge and experience to be able to follow this thorougly, and I got stuck basically all the time.

That's why at the end I thought it was kind of stupid. It felt like they didn't account for the guys that just started in this field. After that we got a group photo and left. I think this was alright, seeing the company was cool, and me not being able to follow the demo/workshop was a bit disappointing, but it isn't deteriorating any sort of motivation.
