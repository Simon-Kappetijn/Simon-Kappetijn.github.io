It's always 'Who's Simon?', but never 'How is Simon?'"

Hi I’m Simon, I’m 17 years old and my residency is in Dreischor, Schouwen-Duiveland (you can see a video on the right). I’m a very social person, I enjoy just talking, and going on with it for hours. As the previous sentence suggests, I’m also a very deterministic person. Once I enjoy doing something, I like to give it my all and seeing myself and my progress improve in that. My whole personality is basically just being social, so I also like playing team sports, for the goofs and gaffs but also because it’s just fun to move around. I also chose HBO-ICT because of this. I already had a slight interest in programming and making stuff, but the team aspect of it also sparked an interest in me, since I think it’s very fun to work towards something as a team, achieve something, and of course at the end celebrate it.

I have a few hobbies, namely playing different kinds of ballsports, like baseball, football and badminton. But the biggest of them all, probably not a surprise, is video gaming. I've played a bunch of 'em, my favourites probably are:

  • Minecraft, pretty standard
  • Valorant/Counter Strike
  • Blade and Sorcery
  • Quaver (video on the right)
